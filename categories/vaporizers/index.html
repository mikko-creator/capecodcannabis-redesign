What is Live Resin (in vapes)?

Live resin vape oil is made from freshly harvested plants that are frozen right away to preserve natural flavor and aroma. The extraction process captures a wide range of compounds, resulting in richer flavor, stronger aroma, and a more full-spectrum experience. Live resin cartridges are often chosen by those who value taste and a true-to-plant profile.

What is Distillate (in vapes)?

Distillate vape oil is refined to isolate specific compounds, creating a highly purified, consistent oil. It typically has very little natural flavor on its own, so flavor profiles are often added. Distillate cartridges are popular for their potency, smooth vapor, and reliability, making them a great everyday option.
