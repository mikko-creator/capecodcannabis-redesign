Understanding the Daily Purchase Limit in Wellfleet, MA

Breaking Down Massachusetts’ Daily Purchase Limits for Recreational Cannabis (UPDATED 4/2026)

Massachusetts has a well-defined legal framework for recreational cannabis purchases to ensure consumer safety, responsible usage, and regulatory compliance. The daily legal limit is 56 grams (2 ounces) of flower, but this can be distributed across various cannabis products. Here’s how the daily limit translates into practical terms and grams (g) for different product types.

Massachusetts’ purchase limits serve three critical purposes:

• Encourage Responsible Use: Limiting daily purchases helps consumers manage their intake responsibly.

• Prevent Diversion: Restricting quantities reduces the risk of products entering the illicit market.

• Promote Safety: Regulating how much THC a consumer can access in one day helps prevent overconsumption.

• Know Your Preferences: Decide what products suit your needs—flower, concentrates, edibles, or a mix.

• Plan Ahead: If you intend to purchase multiple product types, calculate the total equivalency before checkout.

• Consult the Experts: Dispensary staff can help you understand product potencies and their impact on the daily limit.

Understanding the 56-Gram Daily Limit

The state now allows you to purchase 56 grams of flower or its equivalent in other cannabis formats. Let’s break down how these limits apply:

• Flower: Up to 56 grams (2 ounces).

• Edibles: A total of 1000mg THC, which is roughly 10 packs of 20 servings, each serving containing 5mg of THC.

• Concentrates: Up to 10 grams of cannabis concentrates, such as wax, shatter, or live resin (equivalent to 10 x 1g or 20 x 0.5g packages).

• Tinctures: A total of 10000mg = approximately 10 x 1000mg bottles, depending on the product’s potency and total THC content.

• Pre-roll Joints: 56 pre-roll joints, assuming each joint contains roughly 1 gram of flower.

• Vape Cartridges: Up to 20 x 0.5g cartridges or 10 x 1g cartridges/disposables.
