Trusted by Cape Cod Residents and Visitors Since 2021

Choosing a cannabis dispensary is about more than finding the closest location. It’s about finding knowledgeable people, quality products, and a place you can trust.

Since opening in 2021, Cape Cod Cannabis has proudly served Cape Cod residents and visitors with a commitment to education, exceptional customer service, and responsible cannabis retail. Conveniently located in the Route 6 shopping plaza in South Wellfleet, we welcome customers from Provincetown to Sandwich and beyond, and everywhere in between.

Whether you’re visiting Cape Cod for a weekend getaway or you’re a year-round resident, our goal is simple: provide a welcoming, informative, and comfortable shopping experience while helping every customer make confident, informed cannabis decisions.

Why Trust Cape Cod Cannabis?

At Cape Cod Cannabis, trust is earned through consistency, transparency, and a genuine commitment to our customers.

We believe every customer deserves friendly service, knowledgeable guidance, and access to quality recreational cannabis products in a welcoming environment. Whether you’re exploring cannabis for the first time or have years of experience, our team is here to answer your questions, explain your options, and help you find products that best fit your preferences.

Since 2021, we’ve built lasting relationships with thousands of customers by focusing on education before recommendations, honesty before sales, and exceptional service during every visit.

Education First

Responsible and informative education is at the heart of everything we do.

Cannabis affects everyone differently, which is why we believe informed customers make better decisions. Our knowledgeable team takes the time to answer questions, explain products, discuss different consumption methods, and help customers understand the differences between flower, pre-rolls, edibles, vaporizers, concentrates, tinctures, CBD products, seeds, and more. We also offer our Intro to Cannabis 101 page for your reference.

Whether you’re new to cannabis or an experienced consumer, we’re committed to providing clear, honest information so you can shop with confidence.
