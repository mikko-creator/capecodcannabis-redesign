The History of 420: Cannabis Culture’s Global Celebration

The number “420” has long been associated with cannabis culture. It began in the 1970s when a group of California teenagers used “420” as a code to meet and smoke at 4:20 p.m. Since then, the number has evolved into a worldwide celebration of cannabis, and on April 20th (4/20), the cannabis community comes together to recognize its significance. Here at Cape Cod Cannabis, we embrace 420 as a time to reflect on the progress of the cannabis industry and honor its cultural impact, particularly in Massachusetts.
