Bourne, Massachusetts, is a gateway town to Cape Cod, known for its scenic beauty, historical significance, and relaxed atmosphere. Located at the westernmost point of the Cape, Bourne is the first town visitors encounter when crossing the iconic Bourne and Sagamore Bridges, which span the Cape Cod Canal. The canal is a major feature of the town, offering a variety of outdoor activities such as biking, walking, fishing, and boat-watching along its picturesque paths.

Bourne is home to several charming villages, each with its own unique character. These include Buzzards Bay, the town’s bustling hub; Monument Beach, known for its tranquil sandy shorelines; and Pocasset and Cataumet, which feature quiet residential areas and lovely coastal views. The town’s beaches, such as Scusset Beach and Monument Beach, provide opportunities for swimming, boating, and enjoying the scenic Cape Cod coastline.

Bourne has a rich history that is reflected in its many historic sites and landmarks. The Aptucxet Trading Post Museum is one of the oldest sites in the region, dating back to the 1600s, offering insights into early colonial and Native American trade. The Cape Cod Canal Railroad Bridge, a unique vertical-lift bridge, is a striking landmark that highlights the town’s importance as a transportation hub.

In addition to its natural and historical attractions, Bourne offers a range of recreational activities. The Cape Cod Canal, in particular, is a favorite for cyclists, joggers, and nature lovers, while the town’s golf courses, parks, and marinas provide ample opportunities for outdoor enjoyment.

With its mix of coastal beauty, historic charm, and outdoor activities, Bourne serves as both an inviting gateway to Cape Cod and a peaceful destination in its own right. It’s a perfect place for those seeking a relaxed, scenic experience with easy access to both the mainland and the rest of the Cape.

Recreational = 56 grams (flower) a day is the legal MA limit, which can constitute:

• Flower: Up to 56 grams (2 ounces).

• Edibles: A total of 1000mg THC, which is roughly 10 packs of 20 servings, each serving containing 5mg of THC.

• Concentrates: Up to 10 grams of cannabis concentrates, such as wax, shatter, or live resin (equivalent to 10 x 1g or 20 x 0.5g packages).

• Tinctures: A total of 10000mg = approximately 10 x 1000mg bottles, depending on the product’s potency and total THC content.

• Pre-roll Joints: 56 pre-roll joints, assuming each joint contains roughly 1 gram of flower.

• Vape Cartridges: Up to 20 x 0.5g cartridges or 10 x 1g cartridges/disposables.
